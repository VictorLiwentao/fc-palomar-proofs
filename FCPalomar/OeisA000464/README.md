# OEIS A000464: Bala's conjectures for sin x / cos 2x

Let a(n) = (2n+1)! [x^(2n+1)] sin x / cos 2x (OEIS A000464: 1, 11, 361, 24611, …; Glaisher, 1914). Peter Bala made four conjectures on it in 2021. The package refutes the first as stated: modulo every multiple of 81, (a(n))_{n≥1} is not purely periodic with any period, because a(55) ≡ 65 ≢ 11 = a(1) (mod 81). It proves the corrected form, a(n + φ(k)) ≡ a(n) (mod k) whenever 2n + 1 is at least the exponent of every odd prime in k, so the conjecture holds eventually for every k and from n = 1 when no odd prime exponent of k exceeds 3. It proves the other three conjectures: the Gauss congruences a(np^k + i) ≡ a(np^(k−1) + i) (mod p^k) for every shift i, with the integrality of exp(Σ a(n+i) x^n/n); a(mn) ≡ a(m)^n (mod 2^(2v₂(m)+4)); and a(2mn) ≡ a(n)^(2m), a((2m+1)n) ≡ a(n)^(2m+1) (mod 2^(v₂(m)+4)). Behind them are a(n + 2^(e−1)) ≡ a(n) (mod 2^e) for every n, and a(n + (p−1)p^(e−1)/2) ≡ (−1)^((p−1)/2) a(n) (mod p^e) for odd primes p and 2n + 1 ≥ e. The period can be sharpened from φ(k) to the Carmichael function λ(k): a(n + 2^(e−2)) ≡ a(n) (mod 2^e) for e ≥ 3, so a(n + λ(k)) ≡ a(n) (mod k) under the same condition on n, and λ(8) = 2 cannot be lowered, since a(1) ≢ a(0) (mod 8). Bala's congruence conjectures for exponential generating functions are recent targets in the study of congruences for Euler-type numbers: Kallat proved Bala's conjectures for A158690 (arXiv:2609.07238), and Blanco Gómez refuted the same pure-periodicity wording for the Euler numbers A000364 and proved its eventual form (Zenodo 23150466, Theorem 2.2). The odd-prime congruence is known (Blanco Gómez, Zenodo 23407292, Theorem 1), and the 2-adic period follows quickly from Nishibuchi's criterion (arXiv:2610.11648, Prop. 3.4). No earlier treatment of the failure modulo 81 or of the power congruences 3) and 4) was found.
