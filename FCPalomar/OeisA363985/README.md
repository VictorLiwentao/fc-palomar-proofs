# OEIS A363985 and siblings: Bala's p^(3r) supercongruences for u_m, every integer m

For an integer m let u_m(n) = Σ_{k=0}^{n} (−4)^{n−k} C(n,k) C(mn+k, k) C(2k, k), with the binomial C(mn+k, k) taken as a polynomial in its top. The package proves that u_m(np^r) ≡ u_m(np^{r−1}) (mod p^{3r}) for every integer m, every prime p ≥ 5 and all n, r ≥ 1. This is the u-half of Peter Bala's 2023 conjecture on OEIS A363985. It covers A363985 (m = 2), A363987 (m = 3), A363989 (m = 4) and A362676 (m = −1, up to the sign (−1)^n), each stated as its own theorem. The companion v-half of the conjecture (A363986, A363988, A363990) is not covered. Osburn and Sahu's tower for the generalized Domb numbers needs a factor C(n,k)^A with A ≥ 2. The proof here first writes u_m(N) = Σ_j (−1)^j C(mN, N−j) C(2j,j) C(2N−2j, N−j), where C(mN, N−j) occurs only once. The missing powers of p come from the super Catalan factorization of C(2j,j) C(2N−2j, N−j) and from half-block sums of j^{−2}, as in Osburn–Sahu–Straub's proofs of their Theorems 1.3 and 1.2.
