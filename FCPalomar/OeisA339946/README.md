# OEIS A339946 and A361717: Bala's prime-level supercongruences

Let a_m(n) = [x^(mn)] 1/P_n((1−x)/(1+x)), where P_n is the Legendre polynomial; OEIS A339946 is the case m = 1. The package proves Peter Bala's 2022 conjecture that a_m(p) ≡ a_m(1) = 2 (mod p^5) for every m ≥ 1 and every prime p ≥ 7, and with it his 2020 conjecture a(p) ≡ a(1) (mod p^5) for A339946. It shows that the range p ≥ 7 is optimal: a_m(5) ≡ 2 + 625m² (mod 5^5), so the congruence at p = 5 holds exactly when 5 divides m. It also proves Bala's Conjecture 2 for OEIS A361717: Σ_{k=0}^{p−2} C(p−2, k)² C(p−1+k, k) ≡ 1 − 2p − p² (mod p³) for every prime p ≠ 3. Bala's entry lists A103882 and the Apéry numbers A005258 and A005259 as coefficients of similar Legendre-polynomial expressions that satisfy supercongruences u(np^k) ≡ u(np^(k−1)) (mod p^(3k)), and it presents a(p) ≡ a(1) (mod p^5) as a stronger congruence. A361717 is of the same type: A005258(n) = [x^n] (1−x)^(−1) P_n((1+x)/(1−x)) and A361717(n+1) = [x^n] (1−x)^(−2) P_n((1+x)/(1−x)) (Bala, December 2020 and August 2026). Supercongruences of Apéry-like sequences are a research topic; see Straub (Algebra & Number Theory, 2014) and the references there.
