# OEIS A357559: Bala's divisibility conjectures for k^m-weighted Apéry sums (m = 2, 3)

Let S_m(n) = Σ_{k=0}^n (−1)^(n+k) k^m C(n,k) C(n+k,k)²; S_3 is OEIS A357559 and S_0 is the Apéry sequence A005258. The package proves Peter Bala's 2022 Conjecture 1, n²(2n+1)² ∣ S_2(2n), and the conjecture in the entry's formula section, n²(2n+1)³ ∣ 3·S_3(2n), for every n ≥ 0. Both follow from stronger statements for every n: n²(n+1)² ∣ 2S_2(n), with n²(n+1)² ∣ S_2(n) for odd n, and n²(n+1)³ ∣ 6S_3(n). These give an extra factor 2 in both conjectures. The constants 2 and 6 cannot be lowered: 36 ∤ S_2(2) = 126, 8 ∤ 3S_3(1) = 12 and 576 ∤ 2S_3(3) = 16896. Bala's Conjectures 2 and 3, for m ≥ 4, are not treated. Divisibility theorems for binomial sums of this kind, including weighted sums of Apéry numbers, are a research topic: see Guo and Zeng (Int. J. Number Theory 2011 and 2012) and Guo's proof of a divisibility conjecture of Z.-W. Sun (Electron. J. Combin. 2013).
