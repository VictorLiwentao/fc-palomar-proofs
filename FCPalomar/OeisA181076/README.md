# OEIS A183128, A183129 and four siblings: Hanna's binomial-power exponential conjectures, with his non-integrality notes

For U ∈ 1 + zℤ[[z]] put B(n,k) = [z^k] U(z)^n. Paul D. Hanna conjectured in 2010 that exp(Σ_{n≥1} [Σ_k B(n,k)^(e(n,k)) x^k] xⁿ/n) has integer coefficients for six choices of U and e: B = C(n+k−1,k) with e = k²+1 (A183128), k²+k (A183129), n (A181076) and n+k−1 (A181078), and B = C(n,k) with e = n+k (A181082) and n+k+1 (A181084). The package proves all six, and Hanna's notes that the exponents k² and k²+k−1 (in A183128, A183129) and k (in A181070, with B = C(n,k)) do not give integer series. The six conjectures follow from a general criterion on e, in the style of Dold's congruences along the rays (n,k) → (pn,pk), proved here for the diagonal series and any U; its corollaries cover constant exponents and polynomial exponents under a divisibility condition. A converse shows the non-integrality for a class of exponents. Integrality of exp(Σ c(n)xⁿ/n) is governed by Dold's congruences, which arise in topology and dynamics (see the survey of Byszewski, Graff and Ward, Bull. London Math. Soc. 53 (2021)). The congruence B(pn,pk) ≡ B(n,k) used here is a Gauss congruence for the coefficients of the rational function 1/(1 − yU(z)) when U is rational, in the sense studied by Beukers, Houben and Straub (Acta Arith. 184 (2018)).
