# OEIS A372211–A372213 and A351859: Bala's p^(3k) towers for [x^(rN)] f^(sN), f = (1 − x^m)^m/((1 − x^a)^a (1 − x^b)^b)

For a, b ≥ 1 and m = a + b let f(x) = (1 − x^m)^m/((1 − x^a)^a (1 − x^b)^b) and u_{r,s}(N) = [x^(rN)] f(x)^(sN). The package proves the supercongruences u_{r,s}(np^k) ≡ u_{r,s}(np^(k−1)) (mod p^(3k)) for every prime p ≥ 5 with p ∤ abm, all r ≥ 1, s ∈ ℤ and n, k ≥ 1, and the same for Bala's variant f⁻(x) = (1 − x^m)^m/((1 + x^a)^a (1 + x^b)^b). As corollaries it proves Peter Bala's 2024 conjectures for A372212 ((a, b) = (2, 5), p ≥ 11), A372213 ((3, 4), p ≥ 11, and also p = 5), A372211 ((2, 3), p ≥ 7, both forms), his 2022 conjectures for A351859 (G₃ = (1 + x + x² + x³)⁴/(1 + x + x²)³, p ≥ 5, both forms), and his general clauses for all (a, b) and sufficiently large p, for f and for f⁻ (both read with the power n, as [x^n] f(x)^n and [x^n] f⁻(x)^n). A372211 at p = 5 is not claimed; it is open. For a = b = 1, u_{1,1}(n) = C(2n, n), and the towers are the classical Jacobsthal–Kazandzidis congruences (see Meštrović's survey, cited in A351859). Supercongruences of this strength for diagonals of rational functions are studied for Apéry-like numbers (Straub, Algebra Number Theory 8 (2014)) and for framings of rational functions (Müller, arXiv:2104.10754).
