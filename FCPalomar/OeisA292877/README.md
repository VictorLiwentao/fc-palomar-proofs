# Hanna's parity conjectures for eight power-series entries (OEIS A292877 family)

Each of eight OEIS entries by Paul D. Hanna defines a power series A(x) by coefficient conditions on its powers, such as [x^(n−1)] 1/A(x)^(n²) = 0 for n > 2 (A292877) or [x^n] A(x)^(n²+n+1) = 0 for n > 1 (A230218). For each entry the package proves that the conditions determine A uniquely (with a(0) = a(1) = 1 for the five entries whose conditions leave a(0) and a(1) free), that its coefficients are integers, and Hanna's conjectures on which coefficients are odd: the Fibbinary numbers for A292877 (with its two other conjectures), (4^k − 1)/3 for A303562, 2^k − 1 for A303563, the powers of 2 (for n > 0) for A230218, and 2f + 1 with f Fibbinary (for n > 0) for A171791, A263075, A263190 and A371673. For every integer m, general theorems treat two families with a(0) = a(1) = 1: (I) [x^N] A^(−(N²+(m−1)N+1)) = 0 and (II) [x^N] A^(N²−mN+1) = R_N, for N ≥ 2. Each has exactly one solution. Its coefficients are integers congruent modulo 2 to those of the Fuss–Catalan series T_m = 1 + xT_m^m, in family (II) when 2(N² − mN + 1) divides R_N. The eight entries are cases with m = 2, 3, 4, −1 and −2.
